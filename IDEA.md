# Turntable

Reusable 360° vehicle viewer for DOX dealer and campaign sites. A frame-sequence
turntable over a swappable background, with live recolouring of paint, roof,
mirror caps, trim and wheel accents.

Proven in docs/reference/poc.html (single-file canvas prototype, 12 frames).
Goal: a production-grade, config-driven component that works for any vehicle,
given frames + per-part masks.
